The Clear Picture of HetEx Platform

A complete walkthrough of every core innovation powering our high-volume multi-courier ecosystem.

📱

1. Smart Customer Autofill

Place the recipient mobile number first in the booking form. When it matches any customer in your database, the buyer's full name, address, city, state, and pincode automatically populate, cutting booking time by 75%.

✓ Instant customer phone lookup with zero repeated typing
📦

2. Multi-Item Packaging & Math

Add multiple items per order with custom categories, quantities, declared values, and individual box dimensions. The engine computes dead weight vs. volumetric weight (L×W×H/5000) to ensure accurate carrier billing.

✓ Real-time billable weight calculation to prevent courier disputes
💳

3. Dynamic UPI QR Gateway

Enter any recharge amount to instantly generate a compliant UPI QR code encoded with the platform VPA. After paying, click “I Have Paid”—the request stays “Under Review” until approved by Admin. Never credited before approval.

✓ Admin-configured merchant UPI ID with zero gateway deduction fee
🖨️

4. Thermal 4×6" Labels with Barcode

Generate ready-to-print thermal shipping labels for standard 100×150mm printers. Includes scannable AWB barcodes, consignee & warehouse addresses, order numbers, payment mode, and itemized box specifications.

✓ Multi-item split package support (e.g. Package 1 of 3, 2 of 3)
🛡️

5. Automated NDR & RTO Shield

When a delivery attempt fails (customer unavailable, incorrect address), instant non-delivery alerts trigger. Action reattempts, update delivery address in real time, or initiate return to keep RTO losses under 4.5%.

✓ Real-time delivery exception resolution via carrier APIs
⚖️

6. Seller Portal & Super Admin HQ

A unified experience with role separation: Seller Portal for order manifests, pickups, and tickets; Root Admin Panel for managing courier active statuses, commission margin rules, seller KYC, and helpline settings.

✓ Granular access control with audit trails & zero-alert modal dialogs
